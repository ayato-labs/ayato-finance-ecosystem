# Commercial Licensing & Support

Ayato Finance Ecosystem is a dual-licensed project. While the core is available under the AGPL-3.0 for open-source use, we offer a **Commercial License** for organizations that require closed-source integration, proprietary modifications, or professional support.

## Why a Commercial License?

The AGPL-3.0 is a "copyleft" license that requires you to share the source code of any derivative work if you provide it as a service over a network. A Commercial License removes these requirements, allowing you to:
- Use the ecosystem within proprietary, closed-source products.
- Distribute derivative works without sharing your internal source code.
- Comply with corporate procurement policies that forbid AGPL software.
